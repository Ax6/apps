# Woodblock privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Woodblock doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to us. It doesn't use the microphone, the camera or your location.

## What Woodblock keeps

Your metronome setup, sounds, volumes and settings are stored on your device. So is the delay Headphone sync measured for each pair of headphones you synced.

With Woodblock Practice, your songs and setlists are stored on your device too. The practice log keeps when the click played, for how long, the song that was loaded and the tempos it played at. It records from the first launch, bought or not, so your history is already there when you unlock it.

## iCloud

If iCloud is on for Woodblock, it keeps a copy of your songs, setlists and practice log in your own iCloud account, so they match on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple stores that copy for you, and we can't see it. With iCloud off, everything stays on your device.

## Siri, widgets and the Lock Screen

Woodblock gives Siri the names of your songs, so that "Play Amazing Grace in Woodblock" finds the right one. Apple handles what you say to Siri, as it does for every app. The widgets, the Lock Screen and the Control Center control read the tempo, whether the click is playing and your practice streak from Woodblock on your device.

## Purchases

Woodblock Practice is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Woodblock only learns whether your purchase is active.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Everything stays until you delete it. Deleting a song or setlist in Woodblock also deletes it from your other devices through iCloud. Deleting the app removes what Woodblock stored on your device, and the copy in iCloud stays until you delete it: in the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, open your iCloud storage and choose Woodblock. If you back up your device, the backup includes Woodblock's data, as it does for your other apps.

To stop syncing, turn Woodblock off in the list of apps that use iCloud, under your name and iCloud in the Settings app. Woodblock asks for no permissions, so there are none to take back.

## Children

Woodblock collects nothing from anyone, children included.
