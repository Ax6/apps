# Woodblock support

Email [floorapps@icloud.com](mailto:floorapps@icloud.com) with Woodblock in the subject, and say which iPhone or iPad you have and which headphones, if any. A screenshot helps if something looks wrong.

## Common questions

**What's free?** The whole metronome: every time signature, subdivision and sound, tap tempo, the flash, haptics, headphone sync, the Lock Screen, Control Center, Siri and the Shortcuts app. Opening a lesson from a teacher and playing its pieces is free too. Woodblock Practice adds the speed and gap trainers, listening, songs and setlists that sync between your devices, sending lessons, and the practice log with its widgets. You pay once, with no subscription, and Family Sharing is included.

**The lit beat and the flash land late on my AirPods.** Bluetooth headphones play the click late. Tap the gear on the metronome, then Headphone sync, and tap along with 16 clicks. Woodblock keeps the delay for that pair and uses it whenever they're connected.

**Does the click play with my iPhone on silent?** Yes. It also plays over Music, YouTube or a recording, and they keep playing. A phone call or Siri stops it, and so does disconnecting your headphones, so it doesn't carry on through the speaker.

**How do I start it without opening the app?** Say "Start Woodblock" or "Stop Woodblock" to Siri, or add the Woodblock control to Control Center or the Action button. "Set the tempo in Woodblock" asks for the number. With Woodblock Practice, "Play" and a song's name, then "in Woodblock", loads that song and plays it.

**I don't see Woodblock on my Lock Screen.** It shows while the click plays, with Stop, − and +, and for five minutes after with Play. Make sure Live Activities are on for Woodblock in the Settings app, under Apps, then Woodblock.

**Haptics stop when my iPhone locks.** iOS plays haptics only while the app is on screen, so keep Woodblock open when you practice by feel.

**How do I send a student a lesson?** With Woodblock Practice, put the pieces in a setlist and open it. Tap the share button, choose Send as a lesson, type who it's from and who it's for, add a note to any piece and tap Make lesson. Share the link, or show the code at the end of the lesson and let the student scan it with their phone's camera. A long lesson with long notes can be too big for a code, and then you share the link.

**Does my student need Woodblock Practice?** No. A lesson opens and plays free, each piece as you set it, trainer included. Under each piece a box a day fills in with the minutes the click played it, and Send the week sends you a picture of the boxes with the same numbers as text. Without Woodblock, the link opens a page that shows the pieces, so a parent can see what was set.

**The lesson link says it's cut short.** Part of the link went missing when it was copied, which happens in some emails and chats. Send it again, or let the student scan the code.

**Why does Woodblock ask for the microphone?** Only for listening, part of Woodblock Practice, which shows where your notes land against the beat. It asks the first time you turn listening on. The sound is worked out in memory and never recorded or sent, as the [privacy policy](privacy) says. If you said no, Woodblock offers Turn it on in Settings, or you can turn Woodblock on under Privacy & Security, then Microphone, in the Settings app.

**Which instruments does listening hear best?** Ones whose notes start sharply, like piano, guitar or drums. Bowed, sung and blown notes start softly and read less exactly, and music from another app or another player in the room is heard too. With Bluetooth headphones, Woodblock can't hear its own click to time it, so run Headphone sync first.

**Where does the practice time come from?** From the minutes the click played. Stretches under a minute aren't kept, and stopping for less than ten minutes on the same song carries on the same session, so working on a bar doesn't break it up.

**Can I use a keyboard on iPad?** Yes. Space plays and stops, the arrow keys change the tempo and T taps it.

**My songs don't show up on my other iPhone, iPad or Mac.** Check that both are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud on for Woodblock. Songs and Practice say when iCloud is off. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

**I bought Woodblock Practice on another device.** Tap the gear on the metronome, then Restore purchase.
