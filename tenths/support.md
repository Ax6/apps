# Raincard support

Email [floorapps@icloud.com](mailto:floorapps@icloud.com) with Raincard in the subject, and say which iPhone you have and the town of the place you're asking about. A screenshot helps if something looks wrong.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't Raincard match my gauge?** Raincard's numbers are NOAA's estimates for the spot under your pin, and rain often differs a lot across a few miles. With Unlimited, tap a day to see what the gauges nearby read, and tap Enter your gauge reading to make that day your number, on the card and in every total.

**Why is today's number different from the last 24 hours?** The last 24 hours is radar, up to the latest hour. A day runs from about 7 AM to 7 AM, the way gauge readers count it, and its number comes from NOAA's gauge-checked daily record.

**Why did yesterday's number change?** The newest day starts as a first estimate from NOAA's River Forecast Centers. When the Climate Centers publish the day, their settled estimate replaces it, and tapping the day shows what it was first written as. Hawaii has no first estimate, so its newest day has no number until the Climate Centers publish it.

**It says NOAA's radar is late, or No connection.** Raincard gets its numbers from NOAA's free public services, which don't always answer on time. It keeps what it already has, says which source is late, and asks again when you open it. Pull down on Places to try now.

**Can I add a place in Alaska, Puerto Rico or outside the US?** No. Raincard covers the lower 48 states and Hawaii. NOAA's daily record leaves out Alaska, and Puerto Rico's has decades missing.

**How do I enter my gauge reading?** With Unlimited, tap the day on a place's page or on its card, then Enter your gauge reading. Type what it caught in the units Raincard's Settings show: inches to the hundredth, like 0.25, or millimeters to the tenth, like 6.4. Type 0 if it was dry. If a number looks like a slipped decimal point or the wrong unit, Raincard asks which you meant. To change or remove a reading, tap the day again.

**Does snow count?** Yes, as the water it melts to.

**What's a water year?** A year that runs October to September and takes the year it ends in. To switch, tap the year at the top of the card and pick Water year.

**How do I share a year?** Open the card, tap Share, then Share as PDF or Share as CSV. The CSV has a row for each day with its rain, where the number came from, NOAA's estimate and your gauge.

**The morning note doesn't come.** Turn on Morning note in Raincard's Settings, and make sure notifications and Background App Refresh are on for Raincard in Settings, then Apps, then Raincard. iOS decides when Raincard may check, so the note comes some time after 7 AM and the time varies. If iOS doesn't let it check before noon, that morning has no note. A dry morning sends nothing.

**Which place does a widget show?** Rain at a place shows your first place until you choose another: press and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and pick the place. Rain at your places shows your first four places, or two on the Lock Screen, in the order they're in on Places. To reorder them, drag a place, or press and hold it and tap Move up or Move down. Widgets show what Raincard last fetched and change when Raincard gets new numbers.

**What's free?** One place, with its last 72 hours, the last 30 days, this year's card, the morning note and the small widget. Raincard Unlimited, paid once, adds every place, every year back to 1951 with the PDF and CSV, the gauges nearby, your own gauge, this week's watering, the map and every widget.

**I bought Unlimited on another iPhone.** Open Settings in Raincard and tap Restore purchase. Family Sharing is included.
