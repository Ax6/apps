# Tracewell support

Email [floorapps@icloud.com](mailto:floorapps@icloud.com) with Tracewell in the subject, and say which iPhone you have. A screenshot helps if something looks wrong.

## Common questions

**The photo doesn't land on my paper.** Tracewell finds paper that's lighter than the table under it, like white paper on wood. On a glass table, a white table or a bare wall, use Stand with your phone resting on a glass.

**I bought the unlock on another iPhone.** Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase.

**Where are my time-lapses?** In the Photos app, under Recents.
