# Plinth privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Plinth doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

## The camera

Plinth uses the camera to take photos of the items you sell. The photos are kept inside Plinth on your iPhone and aren't saved to your Photos library until you export them.

## Your photos

You import photos with the iOS photo picker, and Plinth sees only the ones you choose. Plinth cuts each product out on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device tools, and no photo leaves your iPhone unless you export or share it. Your items, their photos and cutouts, your styles, your shop name and your logo are all stored on your iPhone.

## Exporting

When you export to Photos, Plinth asks for permission to add to your library, which doesn't let it see the photos already there. If you turn on "An album for each item" in Settings, Plinth asks for full access to your library, which it uses only to make those albums and put your finished photos in them. When you share photos instead, they go only where you send them.

To count your free photos, Plinth keeps a short fingerprint of each photo it has counted, whether you exported it in Plinth or with the Shortcuts action below. The fingerprint isn't the photo itself, and it means the same photo isn't counted twice.

## Shortcuts

The Make listing photos action works on your iPhone with the photos you give it.

## Purchases

The unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Plinth only learns whether the unlock is active.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Plinth stores stays on your iPhone until you delete it. Items and photos you delete in Plinth wait in Recently deleted for 30 days, then Plinth removes them for good. Delete exported items in Settings removes items whose photos you've all saved or shared for good right away, to free space. Deleting the app removes everything Plinth stored, except two small records in your iPhone's keychain: how many free photos you've used, and the fingerprints above. iOS keeps those after an app is deleted, so reinstalling Plinth doesn't start the free photos over. Neither holds a photo or anything about you. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes Plinth's data, as it does for your other apps. Photos you exported stay in your Photos library, or wherever you shared them, until you delete them there. To take back camera or Photos access, go to Settings, then Apps, then Plinth.

## Children

Plinth collects nothing from anyone, children included.
