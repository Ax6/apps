# Plinth support

Email [floorapps@icloud.com](mailto:floorapps@icloud.com) with Plinth in the subject, and say which iPhone you have. A screenshot helps if something looks wrong.

## Common questions

**The cutout missed part of the product, or kept some of the background.** Tap the photo, then Cutout. Brush with Restore to bring a part back, or with Erase to take background away. Pinch to zoom in for fine edges like chains or lace.

**A photo says "No product found".** Plinth couldn't tell the product from what's around it. Tap Edit and brush over the product. Next time, shoot it on a plain surface in even light, with nothing else in the frame.

**My shop name doesn't show on eBay, Amazon or Mercari photos.** Those marketplaces don't allow logos or names on listing photos, so Plinth leaves it off for them.

**There's an extra photo in my Etsy export.** Etsy asks for one unedited photo in every listing, so Plinth adds each item's first photo as you took it. It doesn't use a free photo.

**Exporting again used up free photos.** It shouldn't. Each photo counts once, so exporting it again for another marketplace is free.

**An export paused.** Your iPhone may be low on space. Free some up, then tap Try again, and the export carries on where it stopped.

**I bought the unlock on another iPhone.** Open Settings in Plinth and tap Restore purchase.

**Where are my exported photos?** In your Photos library, or in an album for each item if you turned that on in Settings. Tap Open when an export finishes to go straight there.
